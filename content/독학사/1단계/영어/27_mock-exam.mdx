## 실전처럼 풀기 위한 준비

이 모의고사는 25문항으로 구성했습니다. 독학사 1단계 영어는 회차마다 정확한 시험 시간이 다를 수 있으므로 여기서는 일반적인 객관식 영어 시험의 기준인 문항당 1분 30초 안팎을 기준으로 삼아, 25문항을 40분 이내에 끝내는 것을 목표로 연습하는 것을 권장합니다.

| 영역 | 문항 번호 | 문항 수 |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 어휘 | 1–5 | 5문항 |
| 문법 | 6–14 | 9문항 |
| 독해 | 15–20 | 6문항 |
| 생활영어 | 21–25 | 5문항 |

풀이 순서 전략으로는, 확신이 서는 어휘·문법 문항을 먼저 빠르게 처리해 시간을 벌고, 독해 지문은 문제를 먼저 훑어본 뒤 지문에서 근거를 찾는 방식으로 접근하는 것이 효율적입니다.

다음 지문을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.

> Last spring, a mid-sized software company tried a four-day work week for three months, without reducing employee pay. Under the new schedule, most teams worked Monday through Thursday and took Friday off, though a few customer-support employees rotated their day off to keep the office covered throughout the week. Before the trial began, managers worried that a shorter week would mean less work getting done, so they tracked project deadlines closely during the trial. To their surprise, most teams met their deadlines at roughly the same rate as before, and a company survey found that employees reported feeling less tired and more focused during the four days they worked. A few managers noted that meetings became shorter and more organized, since teams no longer had an extra day to postpone decisions. Not every part of the company benefited equally: the customer-support team said the rotating schedule made it harder to coordinate handoffs between shifts. After reviewing the results, the company decided to keep the four-day schedule for most departments while giving the customer-support team the option to return to five days if they preferred.

다음 지문을 읽고 물음에 답하시오.

> A city government recently began allowing residents to keep beehives on apartment rooftops, reversing a decades-old rule that banned beekeeping within city limits. The change came after a small group of hobbyist beekeepers presented data showing that urban bee populations had been declining even faster than bee populations in rural areas, partly because city residents had no legal way to help. Under the new rules, residents must register their hives with the city, keep them at least three meters from the edge of a roof, and complete a short training course on hive safety. Within the first year, more than two hundred rooftop hives were registered, and city parks reported a noticeable increase in flowering plants being pollinated. Some residents initially worried about being stung while using nearby balconies, but city officials said reported incidents remained very low because bees generally avoid humans unless their hive is directly disturbed. Local honey producers have also begun selling rooftop honey at weekend markets, giving the program an unexpected economic benefit alongside its environmental goal.
