## 독해 지문 (새로 작성)

### 지문 1 — 공지문

> Riverside Community Center will be closed for renovation from March 3 to April 20. During this period, most regular programs, including the yoga class, the children reading club, and the evening computer class, will be suspended. However, the outdoor basketball court and the small library annex next to the main building will remain open, since the renovation work only affects the interior of the main building. Residents who have already registered for spring programs will receive a full refund automatically within one week, and there is no need to submit a separate request. After the renovation, the center plans to open a new fitness room on the second floor, along with an expanded parking area at the rear entrance. The center apologizes for any inconvenience this closure may cause and encourages residents to check the community bulletin board for updates on the reopening schedule.

### 지문 2 — 이야기(경험담)

> When Maria opened her small bakery five years ago, she believed that the secret to success was simply making the best bread in town. She spent countless hours perfecting her sourdough recipe, testing different flours and fermentation times until the crust came out exactly right. Yet after the first year, sales barely covered the rent. It was not until a regular customer suggested that she host a free weekend workshop, teaching neighbors how to bake at home, that things began to change. At first, Maria worried that teaching people to bake would only reduce her own sales. Instead, the workshops brought in new customers every week, many of whom returned not to buy the bread they had learned to make, but the bread they still did not have time to make themselves. Maria now realizes that sharing knowledge did not compete with her business; it built the trust that her business depended on.
